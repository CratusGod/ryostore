# Byobu

A Super+Esc sidebar for Ryoku shaped like a folding screen standing against the right edge. Time and date mark its head, while fine ruled folds divide your vitals, connections, power, media and levels in the order you chose.

The action row stays at the foot while the middle scrolls, and Wi-Fi, Bluetooth and extensions slide over the folds when opened. Its paper, ink, type and motion follow your Ryoku settings; install it from RyoStore -> Sidebars, then pick it in Settings -> Overlays.
