This bar style contains the ported top bar and its phone-shaped Quick Panel
(quick tiles, notifications, the power menu, and the wallpaper board), adapted
for native Ryoku integration: workspace, active-window and keyboard state come
from Ryoku's Wm facade; system state and actions from shell.services; Bluetooth
from Quickshell.Bluetooth; wallpapers from ryogami; and the palette from Ryoku's
Matugen theme. Its settings screen edits only what the barstyle owns and stores
them in ~/.config/ryoku/barstyle-tansu.json.
