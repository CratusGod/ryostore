# Yatagarasu — the Kasugai Kit

The whole kit, presented as one: the crow and her voice.

Yatagarasu is a notification system in two halves, and the
halves are one creature. The scrolls are her voice; the
bird is Yatagarasu.

**The voice** — the Yatagarasu notification design, in the
Notifications category. Every notification arrives as a
Kasugai dispatch scroll: dark glass, a lit priority edge
(LOW cyan, MEDIUM emerald, HIGH amber, CRITICAL rose), the
sender's seal, the message in mono, and a burn line along
the foot that spends the scroll's remaining life. A
critical does not burn — it waits, ember-lit, ACK REQUIRED.

**The bird** — the Yatagarasu Roost plugin, in the Plugins
category (this bundle's item; plugins install from the
Plugins tab). The Kasugai crow roosts on the desktop on her
prism wire, and her eye wears the freshest word: the last
notification's priority colour, the calm prism when the
wire is clear.

Install both halves and she is whole: scrolls rise, and
the eye answers.

On the author's own machine the composition is completed
by a small host patch (in the Yatagarasu repo,
`host-patch/`) that seats the popup stack at the
bottom-right, directly above the roost — the scrolls float
over her head, as her voice and thoughts, because they
are. The design itself honours every column the host
offers, patched or not.
