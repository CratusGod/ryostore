System Console is the "config 10" entry from the community awesome-fastfetch-collection (https://github.com/Chintanpatel24/awesome-fastfetch-collection), MIT licensed, originally by MiZentUi (https://github.com/MiZentUi/dotfiles). It uses only built-in Fastfetch modules and the small built-in Arch logo, so it ships standalone with no external assets. Unmodified except for packaging. Ported further for Ryoku: the OS-age row that shelled out to stat and date was removed, the date format now prints the day of the month instead of the day of the year, the terminal-size row's column and row labels were swapped back into the right order, the colours row was moved inside the Media panel so its swatches no longer overhang the frame, and the sound row now prints the device name and volume instead of the full ALSA node id.

Ported for every Ryoku edition: the small built-in logo now follows the running distribution instead of always drawing Arch's.
