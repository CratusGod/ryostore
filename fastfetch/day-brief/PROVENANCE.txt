Day Brief is the "config 8" entry (configs/8.jsonc) from the community awesome-fastfetch-collection (https://github.com/Chintanpatel24/awesome-fastfetch-collection), MIT licensed. Ported for Ryoku: Dropped the weather 'command' module (curl wttr.in); no shell-out replacement. Translated all labels from Portuguese to English (Dia e Hora->Date & Time, Placa Video->GPU, Fonte->Font, Dispositivo->Audio, Ouvindo->Playing, Cores->Colors, and the rest). Replaced the kitty PNG placeholder logo with the built-in small Arch logo. Reduced three machine-specific disk mounts (/mnt/nvme2tb, /mnt/kingston1tb) to a single root '/' disk. Reduced the four leading blank lines to two. Routed the cyan header/disk accent through the {#keys} slot; the per-section colours stay ANSI names that follow the terminal theme. It ships as a single config file using only built-in Fastfetch modules, with no external runtime assets.

Ported for every Ryoku edition: the small built-in logo now follows the running distribution instead of always drawing Arch's, and the Distro key shows a Linux penguin instead of the Arch mark.
