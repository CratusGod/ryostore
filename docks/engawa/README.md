# Engawa

A dock for Ryoku shaped like the veranda that runs along the edge of a
Japanese house. One plank lies flush with the screen edge and every app sits on
a board of its own: your pinned apps first, then a doubled seam, then whatever
else is running.

- A nail under an app for each open window, up to three. The nail of the app
  you are in turns vermilion and its board darkens.
- Hover an app to lift it and read its name on a paper tag; with magnification
  on, it and its neighbours grow.
- Click to open or switch to an app (again to cycle its windows), middle-click
  for a new window, right-click for Open or New window, Pin or Unpin, and Close.
- With automatic hiding on, the plank slides off the edge whenever a window has
  focus and leaves a hairline; touch it to bring the plank back.

Engawa follows the settings every Ryoku dock shares: the screen edge (it works
on all four), icon size, automatic hiding, magnification and your pinned apps.
Its colours and type come from your colour scheme, and reduced motion stills its
movement.

Install it from RyoStore -> Docks, then pick it in Edit desktop -> Dock.
