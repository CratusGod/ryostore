This app launcher is the Tansu launcher: the phone-shaped home screen with the
app grid, the search box and its cards (calculator, clipboard, colour, emoji and
web search) and the system actions, together with the wallpaper board it turns
into. It is adapted for native Ryoku integration: apps come from Quickshell's own
DesktopEntries index, session actions (lock, sleep, log out, reboot, shut down)
from Ryoku's SessionActions, Wi-Fi from Ryoku's Network, Bluetooth from
Quickshell.Bluetooth, night light from Ryoku's Nightlight, and wallpapers from
ryogami; the palette is Ryoku's Matugen theme. Its settings live in
~/.config/ryoku/launcher-tansu.json.
