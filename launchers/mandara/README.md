# Mandara

The Jurai suite's launcher for Ryoku — the Obelisk herself,
ported whole from Jurai shell. Summon her, type, run.

She is a centred sentient modal: a smoked-glass plate with a
prism rim in her morph colour, her author's name at her head
beneath the Jurai emblem, and the lens — one input that
hears everything.

**She drives in three breaths.** Everything in her fits
three houses, and a breath is a word — type `sun`, `moon`
or `celestial`, or lock the breath as a chip, and she
changes how she drives:

- **☀ SUN — doing.** Amber. The bench of making and work
  rides first, and the filesystem road opens to plain lines.
- **☽ MOON — living.** Indigo. The daily round: frecency
  pure, exactly as she ranks at rest. She wakes in Moon.
- **✧ CELESTIAL — playing.** White prism. Games and media
  float up; the evening is hers.

The breath tints her water, warms her stamp, speaks in her
placeholder, and persists between summons.

**What the lens resolves:**

- **APPLICATIONS** — your desktop entries, matched and
  ranked by her own scoring (exact, prefix, contains —
  and what you run often rises, because she remembers),
  leaned by the breath she is driving in.
- **SEGMENTS** — her runes, speaking the daily tongue now.
  Type a segment's name and the rest of the line rides as
  its argument: `open` and `find` (Sun), `ask`, `note`,
  `timer`, `clip`, `lock`, `sleep`, `vol`, `mute`, `wifi`,
  `bright` (Moon), `play`, `pause`, `next`, `prev`, `shot`
  (Celestial). **TAB locks a segment** as a crystallized
  chip; chips and the line compose in her grammar, and the
  portal's suggestions are dynamic — kin of a locked house
  lead, and your own usage outranks the dictionary.
- **FILESYSTEM** — a path-shaped query (`/…`, or `files …`)
  finds under your home. Enter opens. Under Sun, any line
  searches files beside apps.
- **RASHIN** — a line that is natural language becomes
  *Ask Rashin*. Enter opens the compass window and asks
  Ryoku's real Rashin service, using its configured route
  and shared Ask history.
- **COMMAND** — a line nothing answers to becomes a command
  row. Enter runs it in the shell. Type `abyss` and she
  becomes a shell passthrough until you say it again.

The keyboard is a grammar, not a shortcut list: ↑↓ choose,
Ctrl+J/K ride the same road, Page and Ctrl+↑↓ jump sections,
Ctrl+1–9 casts the nth answer outright, TAB locks a segment
or takes the ghost, Esc peels — radial, chips, line, then
her. The Guardian stands at her gate, as she always did: a
bad IPv4 octet, a mistyped flag, a line that fights itself —
she raises her badge and stops the execute until the line
is fixed. A target in the line raises the holo ring, and
TAB on it blooms the radial diamond.

Application, file, and command resolution stays local.
Questions use Ryoku's configured Rashin service. Mandara's
own memory lives in `~/.local/state/ryoku/mandara-usage.json`
and `mandara-drive.json`.

Install (RyoStore): the `mandara` app launcher, then pick
her in Settings -> App Launcher. Super+Space summons her
from then on.
