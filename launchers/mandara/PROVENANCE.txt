Mandara — provenance

Her face crossed whole. This product is the Obelisk — the
centred sentient modal of Jurai shell's Mandara apsara —
ported as she stands, in the order her author set: port
first, gut second, grow third. Nothing about her was
redesigned to resemble any other launcher; her signature
is the contribution.

THE PORT'S HAND — every touch, declared:
  * Main.qml is new: her own SiMLauncher window grammar
    (full-screen seat, the world dimmed to 0.45 over 300ms,
    click-away retires, exclusive keys) wearing Ryoku's
    launcher contract (shown / show / hide / toggle /
    stateDump), her entrance a breath out of 0.98.
  * runes/SiMController.qml is rewritten under her own name
    and her exact public shape: same sections (INTENTS ·
    APPLICATIONS · FILESYSTEM), same transforms, her scoring
    kept (exact 1000 · prefix 500 · contains 100 · usage
    × 25; an intent row keeps the priority she gave it).
    Sources re-pointed: DesktopEntries + AppLaunch for apps,
    a plain find under the home for files, her RuneRegistry
    for intents, and a line nothing answers to becomes a
    command row — her road, not a conversation.
  * tw/SiMOrchestrator.qml is a new singleton under the name
    the face already speaks to: her accents and prismatic
    chain with her own values (the Obelisk's mode feeds it,
    as it always did), her usage memory kept in her own
    state file, her keystroke tempo measured locally, and
    the core's telemetry resting at zero, labelled resting.
  * Obelisk.qml carries surgical edits only: the qs.* and
    roms imports gone (SapOrb and Typography now ride the
    product's own components module); the ROM host, the war
    bay, the One Law gate, the ledger verbs, the song fetch
    and the core wires deleted; the dispatch reduced to the
    controller's road; April's translations (which named the
    hunt roads) rest empty; the header's telemetry line
    drops its ROM clause; the stamp drops its ROM branch.
    The Guardian's local watch — bad octets, flag typos,
    collisions — stands untouched, as do the abyss rite,
    the echoes, the tracks, the radial diamond, the portal.
  * InputLens.qml: two call sites — the registry answers
    where the neural classifier did (classify / ghostText).
  * RuneRegistry.qml: three functions appended (classify,
    ghostText, suggestions — the portal and pills read them
    where they read NLU.suggestions); the catalog itself is
    untouched, her vocabulary as she kept it. The neural
    classifier's per-tool preset tables did not cross.
  * SiMResultsList.qml: the qs.core import and Apps.iconFor
    severed to the host's Icons.path.
  * components/Theme.qml + Typography.qml are new resting
    singletons (her eye, her phantom, the deep; Orbitron —
    the shell config's own default for fonts.technical).
  * The qmldir files are new (the greeter law: a module's
    singletons are declared, or they are not there).

An earlier v1.0.x of this product was a lean placeholder
plate built to prove the launcher plumbing. It stood down
the day the port began and is replaced whole by this face.

    gone; her orb's energy and the stamp's lamp now read her

    "@ — applets…", and the reference card's @inseicomm row
    is struck.
  * VitalsBar.qml is removed: nothing in the launcher ever
    seated it — dead cargo from the core's window.
Historical comments that name Insei as history stand; only

ADDENDUM — the fall, corrected (2026-10-25, her author's
word, v2.1.1): the kinetic embers and sparks now fall from
directly beneath the cursor's current position in the lens
— input is what triggers them, so input's seat is their
origin. The spawn point is the caret rectangle mapped fresh
into the canvas at each keystroke (a cached, offset guess
stood there before, and the sparks rained from random
widths and edges). Embers are born just under the glyph
line and fall; sparks are born at the same seat, faster,
and gravity takes them too.

ADDENDUM — the fall, tuned to be seen (2026-10-25,
v2.1.3): measurement (caret markers framed against the
live caret) proved the spawn point true — what lied was
the fall. The particle clock ran at double time and
gravity flung each ember to the lens's floor in a blink,
where the strip's edge clipped it: the eye caught flashes
at the bottom border, not a fall from the caret. Now the
physics run honest (one frame, one dt), the fall starts
slow at the caret's foot and gathers gently, and the
canvas outgrows the lens downward so the fall continues
over the plate and fades instead of clipping. The trigger
moved to the caret itself — cursorPositionChanged — so
the embers follow the input cursor's motion, arrows and
clicks included, exactly as her author always intended.

ADDENDUM — her keyboard, made intuitive (2026-10-25,
v2.3.0, her author's word): the port had left the lens's
arrow and number signals unconnected — selection moved
only under the mouse, the caret's own Left was eaten, and
Escape retired her mid-line. All of it is wired now, in
the grammar hands already know: ↑↓ (and Ctrl+J/K, Ctrl+N/P)
walk the rack; Page and Ctrl+↑↓ jump between sections;
Ctrl+1–9 casts the nth entry outright, portal first when
she is open, as the badges always promised; Escape peels
— radial, then chips, then the line — and only retires
her from an empty seat; Left/Right belong to the caret
again, with Ctrl+arrows walking words. The Grimoire
passthrough that held Ctrl+C/L/U is stood down (the
launcher keeps no PTY): Ctrl+C is copy, Ctrl+U clears the
line, Ctrl+L clears line and chips, Ctrl+W eats the word
behind the caret, Ctrl+A/E ride to the line's ends. The
stamp at her foot teaches the grammar in two states, at
rest and while a line stands.

ADDENDUM — v2.4.0, the Three Breaths and the daily tongue
(2026-10-25, his word). Everything in her fits three houses
now. Sun is doing — the bench of making and work. Moon is
living — the daily round, the house's small levers, the
compass door. Celestial is playing — media and capture and
delight.

The breaths are driving modes, and a mode is a word: type
"sun", "moon" or "celestial" (or lock the breath as a chip)
and she changes how she drives — her water takes the
breath's colour (the prismatic chain, under the modality
flashes), her stamp names the breath, her placeholder speaks
it, Rashin's frame drinks the same tint, and her ranking
leans: Sun warms the work-and-making bench and opens the
filesystem road to plain lines; Celestial floats games and
media; Moon stays pure frecency. The mode persists in
~/.local/state/ryoku/mandara-drive.json — she wakes in the
breath she slept in.

The rune segment system came home from the hunt. The
catalog is daily now, written by house: Sun carries open,
edit, term, find; Moon carries ask (the Rashin door), note
(~/Notes/inbox.md), timer, clip, lock, sleep, vol, mute,
wifi, bright; Celestial carries play, pause, next, prev,
shot; the breaths themselves are segments too. A segment is
data — a word, a house, a template; "{arg}" takes the rest
of the line, takesArg:false stands alone, and the door
segments (open, find, ask, the breaths) act in the Obelisk
instead of assembling. The portal's suggestions are dynamic:
kin of a locked house lead, and his own usage outranks the
dictionary. The reference card teaches the three houses.
The hunt catalog was not deleted from the record — it
stands in this repo's git history, whole; it simply stops
being her mother tongue.

Cut at his word in the same pass: the Akashic echoes (her
keeping of past lines, and the strip that ghosted them) and
the Lucid pills row. The Lucid portal remains — it is how
suggestions are picked; the pills were a second, lesser
mouth for the same feed.
